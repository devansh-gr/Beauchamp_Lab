We study how the nervous system generates and controls movement, using spinal motoneurons (
the output layer
of the neuromotor system) to estimate upstream signaling from spinal, cortical, and subcortical sources. We translate that understanding into technologies for rehabilitation, assistive devices, and human-machine interfaces.
