## Recurring patterns in the best ones
1. Research question as the headline, not the lab name.
2. One live interactive data object on the homepage.
3. Nav mirrors visitor intent: Research, People, Publications, Join, plus Data/Code/Demos.
4. Flagship project cards: thumbnail, one-liner, outbound link.
5. Stats band with real numbers.
6. Real Publications page, typed, with citation export.
7. Join page with explicit expectations and trainee voices.
8. Dated, typed news feed.

## Anti-patterns
1. Typos and template leftovers ("(c) 0000", "Almuni"). Ours: "Likes:" blanks, "Coming soon".
2. No publications page (Jazlab, Kording, current beauchamplab.org).
3. Dead domains: bensmaialab.org now redirects to an unrelated .tv site. Keep auto-renew on.

No lab in the exact subfield (HD-sEMG, motor units: Heckman, Negro, Enoka) has a standout site.
That is the opening: be the first motor-unit lab whose site shows motor units.
